# Future Office Setup Ideas

_Last updated: 2026-08-30_

This is a planning document, not a commitment to buy anything immediately. The goal is to build a cleaner, more comfortable office after the move into the larger room later in 2026, ideally while staying within a sensible budget.

## Priorities

1. Large electric sit/stand desk with enough depth for a gaming/programming setup.
2. Comfortable ergonomic chair suitable for long gaming/coding sessions.
3. Clean cable management and a more permanent layout than the current homemade standing desk.
4. Reuse existing PC, monitors, peripherals, and external SSDs unless testing shows an actual need to upgrade.
5. Favor value and durability over cosmetic extras.

## Desk target

### Preferred size

- **Ideal:** about 72 x 30 inches.
- **Minimum worth considering:** about 60 x 30 inches.
- 30-inch depth is strongly preferred over 24-inch tops because it leaves more room for monitor distance, keyboard/mouse space, and accessories.
- Dual-motor frame preferred for a large desktop.
- Memory height presets are useful but not mandatory.
- Stability at standing height matters more than maximum advertised lifting capacity.

### Option A — best budget/value DIY route

**VIVO dual-motor frame + separate large top**

- Current frame pricing observed around **$200-$250** depending on model/retailer.
- Pair with a 60-72 inch x ~30 inch butcher-block, solid-wood, or quality laminate top.
- A local/used top or sale-priced butcher block can make this substantially cheaper than a premium complete desk.
- This route allows a larger and nicer top to be upgraded later without replacing the motorized base.
- Target total budget: roughly **$350-$500**, depending heavily on the top.

This is probably the first route to investigate when it is time to buy because it fits the goal of a large desk without paying premium complete-desk pricing.

### Option B — simpler complete-desk route

**FlexiSpot E7 72 x 30**

- Dual-motor frame.
- Large 72 x 30 configurations are available.
- Current 72-inch E7 listings have been seen around **$490** on sale, though pricing varies substantially by top and retailer.
- More expensive than a bargain DIY build, but less sourcing/assembly guesswork.
- Strong candidate if a good sale appears near purchase time.

### Option C — used-market wildcard

Before buying new, check Facebook Marketplace/local office liquidation for:

- UPLIFT 72 x 30
- FlexiSpot E7/E7 Pro
- VIVO dual-motor desks
- Commercial sit/stand desks

Large desks are expensive to ship and awkward to move, so local used pricing can occasionally be dramatically lower than new retail.

## Chair target

A chair should be tried in person when practical because fit is highly individual. Prioritize seat depth/width, adjustable lumbar support, arm height, recline behavior, and whether the headrest actually lands behind the head rather than the shoulders.

### Budget first choice — Staples Dexley

- Current observed price: roughly **$155-$170** on sale.
- Mesh seat/back, adjustable headrest, lumbar support, adjustable arms, tilt tension and tilt lock.
- Community feedback generally treats it as one of the stronger sub-$200 value chairs, especially for taller/larger users.
- Main caveat: recline/upright geometry is divisive, so it should ideally be tested at Staples before buying.

### Alternative — Staples Hyken

- Current observed price: roughly **$155** on sale.
- Very popular budget ergonomic chair.
- More compact fit than the Dexley; the regular Hyken headrest is frequently criticized by taller users.
- Keep as an option only after sitting in one first.

### Better-chair future upgrade strategy

If the budget later grows to roughly $300-$600, compare the best current new ergonomic chairs against **used/refurbished commercial chairs** such as Steelcase, Herman Miller, or HON before spending the money. A quality used commercial chair may be a better long-term purchase than a new mid-tier consumer chair.

## Future setup details to consider

- Monitor arms to free desktop space.
- Under-desk cable tray.
- Power strip mounted under the desktop so only one main power cable needs to move with the desk.
- Enough cable slack for full standing height before securing cables.
- PC tower location: desktop, dedicated stand, or under-desk mount; keep airflow unobstructed.
- Large desk mat.
- Good task/ambient lighting.
- Optional anti-fatigue mat for standing sessions.
- Consider casters only if the chosen standing frame is rated for them and remains stable.

## Purchase timing

No immediate purchase is required. Re-check prices, sales, used inventory, and chair availability close to the actual office move. Current prices in this file are only an August 2026 snapshot and should not be treated as permanent.
